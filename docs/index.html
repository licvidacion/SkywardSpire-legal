Frequently asked

Everything you might want to know before your first climb.

Is Skyward Spire free?
Yes — it’s free to play. Optional ads and cosmetic block skins help support development, but you can climb as high as you like without spending a thing.
Do I need an account to play?
No account is required. Sign in with Google Play Games if you’d like cloud-saved progress and a spot on the global leaderboards — otherwise everything stays on your device.
Does it work offline?
Absolutely. The full game plays offline. An internet connection is only needed for leaderboards, daily quests and the occasional ad.
