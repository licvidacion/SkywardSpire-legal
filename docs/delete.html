Data & Account Deletion

Skyward Spire (com.mandras.skywardspire) · Mandras Entertainment

Skyward Spire does not require an account with us. Most data stays on your device, and some is processed by Google services (Play Games, AdMob, Firebase). You can delete your data at any time using the options below.

Fastest way: uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device. To also remove cloud-saved progress, delete this game’s data in the Google Play Games app (see step 2).

What data exists

  • On your device: game progress, settings, collection.
  • Google Play Games cloud save (only if you signed in): your saved progress.
  • Analytics & crash data (Firebase): pseudonymous usage and crash logs.
  • Advertising identifiers (AdMob): used to show ads.

How to delete it

  1. Uninstall the app — removes all on-device progress, settings and collection.
  2. Cloud save (Play Games): open the Google Play Games app → Settings → delete this game’s data, or delete your Play Games account data. See Google’s guide.
  3. Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device Settings → Google → Ads.
  4. Request full deletion: email skywardspire.privacy@gmail.com from the address concerned, subject “Data Deletion Request”. We action it (and ask our providers to do the same) within 30 days.

What is kept, and for how long

After a deletion request we remove data we hold. Aggregated or anonymised analytics that can no longer identify you may be retained. Providers (Google) retain data per their own policies; analytics data is otherwise retained for up to 24 months.
