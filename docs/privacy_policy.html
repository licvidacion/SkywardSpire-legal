Privacy Policy

Skyward Spire (com.mandras.skywardspire) · Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you play Skyward Spire (the “Game”). The Game is not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Collected automatically

  • Device type, operating system and version
  • Unique device / advertising identifiers
  • Game progress and usage statistics (e.g. scores, levels)
  • Crash logs and performance data
  • Language and region settings

1.2 Through ads

Our advertising partners may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (GAID/IDFA)
  • IP address (used to estimate general region, not precise GPS)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

1.3 What we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email or contact details
  • Precise / GPS location
  • Photos, contacts, microphone or other personal files
  • Payment card information (handled by Google Play)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Operate, maintain and improve the Game
  • Serve personalised or non-personalised ads
  • Provide optional leaderboards, achievements and cloud saves
  • Analyse performance and fix bugs
  • Prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (ads) — policy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics) — policy
  • Google Play Games Services (sign-in, leaderboards, cloud save) — policy
  • Google Play Billing (purchases) — policy

4. Advertising & Consent (EEA/UK)

We use Google AdMob. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent (via Google’s certified consent form) before any personalised ads are shown. You can change or withdraw consent any time in the Game’s Settings → Privacy → “Manage Ad Preferences”. Without consent you still see non-personalised ads.

5. In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional purchases (gem packs, “No Ads”). All transactions are processed by Google Play; we do not collect or store your payment information.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Game progress and preferences are stored on your device, and — only if you sign in to Google Play Games — backed up to your Google-managed cloud save. Analytics data is retained for up to 24 months, then deleted or anonymised. You can delete your data at any time — see Data Deletion.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / EEA)

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for personalised ads at any time
  • Object to processing, and lodge a complaint with your local authority

8. Your Rights (CCPA / California)

  • Know what personal information we collect
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information — we do not sell it

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures, and data is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, however.

10. International Transfers

Our providers (Google) may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States, governed by their safeguards.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, an in-Game or store notice.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or to request data deletion: skywardspire.privacy@gmail.com
