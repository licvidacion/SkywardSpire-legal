Privacy Policy

Skyward Spire · Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you play Skyward Spire (the “Game”).

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information collected automatically

When you use the Game, we may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device type, operating system, and version
  • Unique device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID)
  • Game progress and usage statistics (e.g., scores, levels completed)
  • Crash logs and performance data
  • Language and region settings

1.2 Information collected through ads

Our advertising partners may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA/GAID)
  • IP address (used to estimate general location, not precise GPS)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

1.3 Information we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, or contact details
  • Precise GPS location
  • Photos, contacts, or other personal files
  • Payment information (handled by Google Play)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Game
  • Provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements
  • Track game progress and award achievements
  • Analyze game performance and fix bugs
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Advertising

3.1 Ad networks

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to serve ads.

3.2 Personalized vs. non-personalized ads

  • Personalized ads use anonymous data about your interests to show more relevant ads.
  • Non-personalized ads are not based on your interests but may still use contextual information (e.g., time of day, app content).

You can change your ad preference at any time in the Game’s Settings menu.

3.3 Ad partners

Our ad partners have their own privacy policies. Google’s privacy policy can be found at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (e.g., gem packs, “No Ads” option). All purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment information.

5. Data Retention

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Analytics data is retained for up to 24 months, after which it is automatically deleted or anonymized.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / EEA Users)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw your consent for personalized ads at any time
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Your Rights (CCPA / California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information

We do not sell personal information.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

10. Third-Party Services

The Game may contain links to third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy within the Game. Your continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at skywardspire.privacy@gmail.com.

13. Consent

By using the Game, you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you are in the EEA, we will obtain your explicit consent before processing personal data for personalized advertising.
